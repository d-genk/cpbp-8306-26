## When you deploy a new one

1. Follow `deploying_gpt_tutors.md`.
2. Paste the URL into the table above, same day — this is the step that gets
   forgotten, and the link is painful to recover later.
3. Post it to that session's Brightspace page.

## If a link stops working

Editing a GPT keeps its URL, so a dead link usually means the sharing setting
was reset to "Only me," not that the GPT is gone. Check that before rebuilding
anything.
