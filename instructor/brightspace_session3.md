## 3. The participation assignment — please change the format this week

Last week's eleven transcripts arrived in five different formats. One PDF
extracted one word per line; at least four were missing half the student's own
turns; one student spent three turns asking the tutor how to copy the
conversation. See `instructor/transcript_findings.md` §4.

**Set the submission to a text box, not a file upload**, and use this copy:

> **Session 3 participation — due before you leave today.**
>
> Work through the Session 3 tutor. Six problems, about twenty minutes. You will
> be asked to **predict what each line of code does before running it** — and the
> tutor will not move on until you commit to an answer.
>
> **Predicting wrong is fine and expected. Refusing to predict is not.** Credit
> is for engaging with the questions, not for being right.
>
> **How to submit — please follow this, it takes ten seconds:**
> 1. Select the whole conversation and copy it.
> 2. Paste it into a plain text editor (Notepad, TextEdit) to strip the formatting.
> 3. Paste that into the box below.
>
> If the copy loses track of who said what, add `ME:` and `TUTOR:` labels. Do not
> upload a PDF or a screenshot — we cannot read them reliably, and you will be
> asked to resubmit.
>
> Expect to reach all six problems. If you finish early, go back to the ones you
> got wrong and try them in the other language.

The "expect to reach all six" line is deliberate — half of last week's cohort
stopped at about ten minutes believing they were done.
